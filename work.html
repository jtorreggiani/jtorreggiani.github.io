Software Engineering

Most of my career has been spent on the parts of a product that other people's work depends on: the APIs, the data models, and the surfaces that have to stay fast while the system behind them changes.

  • Led the overhaul of Square's Payroll overview page and action center end to end — a team of three moving a legacy JavaScript client to TypeScript React and Protobuf APIs, cutting page loads from 10–20 seconds to under 3. I wrote the engineering design, the pay runs Protobuf API, and the client data-fetching architecture, and the patterns became the basis for the rest of Payroll's React migration.
  • Architected the frontend of Payroll's setup guide, raising the rate at which new sellers sent their first payroll by 10–15%; the implementation became the template other Square teams used for their own setup guides.
  • Spearheaded breaking up Artemis's Rails monolith behind a JSON:API, led development of its React Native app for growers, and wrote the open-source data-fetching and state management library shared across its web and mobile clients.
  • Migrated Verba's flagship course-materials client from Backbone to React, built the student-facing portal, and integrated billing with an enterprise student information system — automating reconciliation for university accounts receivable departments moving millions of dollars.
  • Built a Ruby GIS application for a citizen-science project in Plymouth, England: volunteers photographed trees in the city's central park, the app derived coordinates from the images, and MapboxGL rendered the spatial data.

Engineering Management

I came away from managing convinced that most "technical" problems on a team are really communication problems wearing a disguise, and that the manager's job is mostly to make the real constraints visible to everyone at once.

  • Ran a team of ten as engineering manager at Artemis — interviews, mentorship, training, and performance reviews — and ran the daily meeting that kept priorities aligned across product, customer success, and sales.
  • Refined how that team gathered requirements, estimated work, and handled QA, with engineering effort pointed at platform stability, performance, and UX.
  • Led a team of three at Square through a multi-quarter rewrite of a revenue-critical payroll surface, and reviewed more than 750 pull requests across the Payroll codebase.
  • Coordinate SPEC's distributed contributor teams as collective administrator — engineers, designers, project managers, curators, and researchers working across more than a dozen open-source and research projects.

Technology Program Management

The work I enjoy most sits between teams: figuring out what a program is actually trying to prove, who has to agree for it to land, and what the first measurable step is.

  • Administer SPEC, a nonprofit collective run with full financial transparency from day one: $290K raised and distributed to community members, 28 career placements, 15 completed projects, and 7 partner organizations.
  • Established and ran a two-year partnership between SPEC and the RAND Corporation's Center to Advance Racial Equity Policy, covering microcredentials, socio-cultural gaming research, and a research intern program.
  • Led Square's Payroll free trial experiment end to end — introducing A/B testing with Optimizely and partnering with the SaaS team on subscription flows — driving a ~20% increase in monthly recurring revenue.
  • Joined a stalled cross-team payroll program months behind schedule, restructured it around iterative milestones with frontend and backend built in parallel, and shipped it three months ahead of the revised forecast — cutting onboarding support tickets 20–30%.
  • Delivered an automated, FDA-compliant interactive voice response system for a Fortune 500 pharmaceutical product release — two months ahead of schedule and $15K under budget.

Teaching & Mentoring

Teaching is the thread that runs through everything else I do — most of the projects I'm proudest of exist to get someone else their first real shot at this work.

  • Co-founded SPEC in 2020 with Dr. Rhianna Rogers, after COVID cut the funding for her decade-long Buffalo Project research into how cultural data shapes learning. I covered a student researcher's salary to keep the work going, and we built an organization around it.
  • SPEC has since placed 28 people in careers through paid internships, microcredentials, and game-based learning programs. I've personally donated $80K toward funding those opportunities.
  • Started staff-level DRI office hours at Square, and mentored engineers across teams there — including three through the Socially Responsible Engineering Mentorship Circle, and a junior engineer who went on to ship critical components of the Payroll setup guide independently.
  • Grew Square's Neurodiversity Community from 100 to 1,000 members as Bay Area chair, running events on mental health, LGBTQ+ experience, and psychological safety at work.
  • Guest lectured on environmental physics and sustainable technologies at the State University of New York at Albany.

AI & Machine Learning

I've been interested in AI since college, when it was still a question about minds and meaning rather than a product category. The philosophy background shows in what I work on now: logic programming and symbolic reasoning on one side, AI-assisted development and what it does to how teams build software on the other.

  • Early contributor to Goose, Block's open-source AI agent harness.
  • Built LAM, an abstract machine for logic programming and automated reasoning, in Rust.
  • Contributor to Tau Prolog, a Prolog interpreter for JavaScript.
  • Developing the domain model at Serspace Labs.
